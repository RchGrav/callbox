backmux help panes - panes, windows, places, views and layouts

A pane shows one attached terminal process; the process runs on whether
or not a pane shows it. backmux shell starts with panes: a cleared screen,
your shell in it, chat open, the bar along the bottom. From any attach,
Ctrl-Space Enter adds a new terminal and Ctrl-Space l attaches another
process. Every key is in backmux help keys.

WINDOWS AND PLACES
Nine windows form a 3x3 grid (1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9), and the screen moves
over it in half-window steps: Alt-arrows past the screen's edge slide it,
smoothly unless backmux config set slide off. A window holds up to four
panes, in places A (top left), B (right), C (bottom left), D (bottom right)
and E (the bottom across). Which places are filled is the window's shape:
A, AB, AE, ABC (B tall), ABD (A tall), ACD (A across the top), ABE (E across
the bottom) or ABCD. A place is written 1A to 9E, as the name bars show it,
and names its process for every command: backmux peek 2C.

THE BASE AND ITS VIEWS
Where panes really are is the base layout. Attach, split, swap, move and a
pane leaving change it: a new pane grows its window into the next shape,
and a split halves the active pane (A down: AE; E right: ACD). The screen
shows the base through a view: by name, spread over all nine windows, or
packed four to a window. A view never changes the base; turning it off
shows the base as it was. Swapping works in any view, moving only in the
base. Layout changes never stop a process.

LAYOUTS
A layout keeps the base by name, in ~/.config/backmux/layouts/<name>, as
plain text. Loading one never ends a process: panes not in it are let go,
ended ones start again. The bar shows its name, with * once panes have
moved since; backmux shell opens the default one. Ctrl-Space L opens the
Layouts panel; backmux help layout has the commands.

THE LAUNCHER, NAME BARS AND THE BAR
The launcher (Ctrl-Space l, or a counter on the bar) lists running
terminals, consoles (servers and pipe-input processes, opened in a viewer
pane that leaves no record) and ended processes, to attach, view or start
again. With Info on, each pane's name bar shows its place and state, its
name (click it to rename), and what it runs with its CPU and memory. The
bar shows the counters, your chat name, Info, the grid and its windows,
the view toggles, Zoom, Lock, Slide, ?, the machine's load and the clock;
on a narrow terminal items shorten, then the least useful go.

PROFILES, LOCK AND DETACH
Ctrl-Space n renames the active process and saves its profile; Ctrl-Space ;
saves the profile (its record survives purge), not the layout. A locked
pane passes Alt-arrows to its program; prefix keys still work. Ctrl-Space q
detaches the active pane and leaves its process running; detaching the
last pane returns to your shell. backmux attach <target> reconnects; end
and kill stop processes.

CHAT
Chat lists agent harnesses only (claude, codex, gemini, ...). Ctrl-Space c
opens it or moves the keyboard into it (again: hides it); there, Tab picks
the recipient, Enter sends, Ctrl-S ticks Assist, Esc hides it. Drag its
title bar to move it. backmux help share brings an agent into your app.

See backmux help keys, layout, swap, attach, shell, and config.

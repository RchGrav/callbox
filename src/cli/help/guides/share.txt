[person]
backmux help share - bring an agent into the app you are working on

Keep working in your app while an agent reads its screen, types into it
when you let it, and answers you in the chat window. The agent needs no
setup: backmux tells it what it can do.

BRING AN AGENT IN
    backmux shell                       your shell, with panes and chat
    backmux detach --name coder claude  an agent, in the background

  Start your app as a named process, from the shell:

    backmux call --name app ./my-tui

  The first time in a folder, claude asks to trust it: backmux attach
  coder to answer, then Ctrl-Space q. Then:

    Ctrl-Space c     open chat, or move the keyboard into it
    Tab              pick the agent (chat lists agent harnesses only)
    Ctrl-S           tick Assist, then type and press Enter

  Assist asks the agent to look at the app in the pane you were last in
  and points it to backmux help assist. Its replies show in chat, and it
  reaches you by your chat name. Ctrl-Space p a puts the agent beside the
  app as a pane, to watch both. Your keys and the agent's reach the same
  app: take turns.

REBUILD AND TEST TOGETHER
  Quit the app normally so the agent can rebuild it. When it says the
  build is ready, start the app again from a shell:

    backmux recall app

  recall reuses the recorded command, arguments, and working directory.
  When finished: backmux end coder, then backmux delete coder and
  backmux delete app.

JOIN AN APP YOUR AGENT STARTED
    backmux attach app

  Ctrl-Space q detaches and leaves it running. A new attach replaces the
  previous client; peek, send, and resize keep working while attached.

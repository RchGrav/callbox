backmux help keys - every key in an attached terminal

Press Ctrl-Space, release it, then the key: Ctrl-Space q means that, and
Ctrl-x means hold both. Ctrl-Space twice sends one Ctrl-Space to the
program. --detach-keys changes the prefix and the detach key (q).
Ctrl-Space ? shows the keys after the prefix; in a mode, ? shows its own.

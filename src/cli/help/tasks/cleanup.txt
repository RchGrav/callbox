backmux help cleanup - stop processes and remove their records{!name}

  backmux end app worker                 SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after 5 s
  backmux kill app                       SIGKILL now
  backmux purge app                      remove one ended process and its
                                         log, freeing its name

end keeps the record and log for logs, wait and recall; purge removes
them, and refuses a running process (exit 2): end it first. Purge by
name: a bare backmux purge removes every ended process not kept with
backmux save, other agents' too.

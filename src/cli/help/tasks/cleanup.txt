backmux help cleanup - stop processes and remove their records{!name}

  backmux end app worker                 SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after 5 s
  backmux kill app                       SIGKILL now
  backmux delete app                     remove one ended process and its
                                         log, freeing its name

end keeps the record and log for logs, wait and recall; delete removes
them, and refuses a running process (exit 1): end it first. Delete by
name: backmux purge removes every ended process not kept with backmux
save, other agents' too.

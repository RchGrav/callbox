backmux help delegate - start another agent and give it work{!name}

  backmux detach --name reviewer env -u CLAUDE_CODE_CHILD_SESSION \
      -u CLAUDE_CODE_CHILD_NOTES_DIR claude
  backmux wait reviewer --idle 1.5s      until its screen is still 1.5 s
                                         (exit 7: still busy after 30 s)
  backmux peek reviewer                  ready? Answer a trust or
                                         permission prompt with send -k
                                         Down / -k Enter, peeking between
  backmux msg reviewer "Review src/main.c and report your findings."

The two -u flags: a Claude Code that inherits them takes itself for a
child of this session (no transcript, registered as its child); unset,
the new agent is a session of its own. Any harness works in place of
claude (codex, ...).
Every message carries a header naming you and the reply command, so the
brief need not say how to report back.
[inside]
The reply is typed in here as soon as it is sent, even mid-turn; nothing
needs to poll. backmux msg --received lists what has arrived.
[outside]
The reply waits in your inbox: backmux inbox --wait (backmux help replies).
[all]
Delivered means typed in, not done: peek shows its progress. When done:
backmux end reviewer, then backmux delete reviewer.

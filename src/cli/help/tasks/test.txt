backmux help test - start an app of your own and test it{!name}

  backmux detach --name app --size 80 24 ./my-tui --flag
  backmux wait app --idle 300            until it has drawn
  backmux peek app                       look (backmux help look)
  backmux send app -k Down --wait 300    act (backmux help drive)
  backmux send app q                     quit it the app's own way
  backmux wait app                       its exit code, once it ends
  backmux delete app                     remove it, freeing the name

detach starts it on a terminal of its own; --size keeps results the same
from run to run. A person can watch with backmux attach app. backmux
recall app runs it again as recorded.

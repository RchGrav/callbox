usage: backmux swap <name|id>,<name|id>
       backmux swap <name|id> <name|id>

Two panes trade places in the terminal that shows them, named by their
processes' names or ids: whoever runs it may not see that screen, so there
are no letters or numbers to guess at. Run from inside a pane it acts on that
terminal; from anywhere else, on the one terminal showing panes.

In the terminal itself, Ctrl-Space s swaps the panes on the screen.

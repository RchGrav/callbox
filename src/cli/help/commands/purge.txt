usage: backmux purge [<target>] [-a|--all] [-s|--system] [-u|--user] [--force]

Remove ended calls and their stored records, logs, and screens.
Without a target, sweep eligible calls; with a target, remove that call.

  -a, --all        include stale calls (widens state, not store scope)
  -u, --user       sweep personal calls (the default)
  -s, --system     sweep root-managed calls; invokes sudo if needed
  --force          with a target: remove it even if saved or running
                   without a target: include stale calls, as -a does

With a target, --force stops a running call before removing it. A sweep
never removes saved or running calls, even with --force. The older spelling
purge all is equivalent to a sweep with -a.

Ordinary purge leaves saved calls alone. --name at launch does not save
a call; save and rename do. Removing a record frees its name.
Aliases: rm, prune, drop. To stop a call and retain its record, use end.

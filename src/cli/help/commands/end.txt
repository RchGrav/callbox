usage: backmux end [--print] <target>...

End: SIGTERM, then SIGKILL

Stop each target's process group with SIGTERM. If it remains running after
five seconds, send SIGKILL. Records and logs are retained unless the process
was launched with --rm.

--print displays the signal command without executing it. stop is an alias.
Use kill for immediate SIGKILL, or purge to remove ended process records.

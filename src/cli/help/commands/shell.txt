usage: backmux shell [--name NAME] [--size COLS ROWS] [-e KEY=VALUE]... [--rm]
                     [shell [args...]]

Your shell (or the one you name) on a PTY, under backmux: it keeps running
when you detach, and recall brings it back as a shell. Backmux's flags come
before the shell's name; every word after it is the shell's own (backmux
shell zsh -l). With no shell named: an executable $SHELL, else your login
shell, else /bin/sh.

It is a call like any other: chat and panes are not part of it. Ctrl-P
Ctrl-T brings up chat over whatever you are attached to, and Ctrl-P Ctrl-A
puts another call beside it (Ctrl-P Ctrl-H lists the keys).

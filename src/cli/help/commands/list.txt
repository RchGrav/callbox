usage: backmux list [all] [-s|--system] [-u|--user] [name]

List running calls with their owner, command, age, status, ports, and name;
list all adds the stopped ones. By default, list shows your personal calls.

  all              stopped calls too
  -u, --user       your personal calls (the invoking user's, even under sudo)
  -s, --system     root-managed calls
  -a, --all        both scopes

A name filters the listing. Root defaults to the root-managed store;
root's -a also includes every user's store. Private root-managed calls
appear with 'hidden' owner and command to other users.

For a running-only view across personal and root-managed calls, use ps.

usage: backmux stats <target> [--no-stream]

Show process-group CPU usage, resident memory, and process count. Refresh
once per second until Ctrl-C. --no-stream, or non-terminal stdout, prints
one sample. CPU usage is summed across the group and can exceed 100%.

Resource measurement is implemented on Linux. On macOS, unavailable
measurements appear as zero; they do not establish actual resource usage.

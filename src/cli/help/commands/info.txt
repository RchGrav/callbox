usage: backmux info <name|id>

Everything about a process, running or ended: its name and id, what kind it is
(call, agent, server, detach, shell), its state and exit, the program and
its arguments, the folder recall starts it in, how messages reach it,
whether it is saved, when it started and ended, its environment (names
only: values can be secrets), and where its log and console are.

backmux inspect prints the record itself, as JSON.

usage: backmux ps [-a|--all] [name]

List running processes across your personal and root-managed stores.
-a, --all includes ended processes. A name filters the listing.

The columns are PROCESS ID, COMMAND, CREATED, STATUS, PORTS, and NAMES.
Private root-managed processes show a 'hidden' command. ps takes no scope
flags; use list for --user, --system, and an owner column.
